Home / Services / PF and ESIC
Service

PF and ESIC, handled every month.

Provident Fund and Employees’ State Insurance are the two obligations that most businesses get wrong without noticing. We manage registration, monthly filings and the day-to-day changes to your employee records.

What we do

  • EPF and ESIC employer registration, including employer codes and digital signature mapping
  • Monthly ECR and ESIC contribution filing, with challan and receipt on file
  • UAN generation or activation for new joiners, and e-Pehchan cards
  • Joiner, leaver and exit-date updates each month
  • KYC seeding and corrections for name, date of birth and Aadhaar mismatches
  • PF transfer, withdrawal and claim support, with rejection follow-up
  • ESIC card corrections and sickness, maternity and medical benefit help

What we need from you

  • Wage register and attendance by the 23rd of each month
  • New joiner details and leaver dates
  • Bank and Aadhaar details for employees, with consent
  • Your approval before any filing is submitted

Monthly rhythm

  • Days 1 to 10: inputs collected and checked
  • By the 15th: filings prepared, approved and paid
  • After filing: acknowledgements stored and shown on your dashboard

Home / Services / Contractor audit
Service

Know your contractors are compliant before you pay them.

If a security, housekeeping or pantry vendor misses PF, ESIC or minimum wages, the risk can reach the company that engaged them. We check each vendor every month, before the invoice is released.

What we check, each vendor, each month

  • PF: contributions on the right wages, remittance by the 15th, UAN and KYC status
  • ESIC: staff within the wage limit are covered, challans paid on time
  • Contract labour: licence validity and approved headcount
  • Muster roll and wage register compared with attendance records
  • Tamil Nadu minimum wage by skill category, with payment timing and wage slips
  • Overtime, deductions and advances in line with the register

What you receive

  • A Green, Amber or Red status for each vendor
  • A recommendation for Finance: release, release with action, or hold
  • A short list of what the vendor must correct, and by when
  • The findings recorded for your own audit file

Findings rely on documents supplied by you and your vendors. Payment decisions remain with you.

Home / Services / POSH
Service

POSH compliance, set up properly and kept current.

The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act needs a policy, a committee, visible notices, training and an annual report. Many workplaces meet some of these, but not all.

What we handle

  • Internal Committee constitution, with presiding officer, members and an external member
  • POSH policy drafted or reviewed for your workplace
  • Notices and policy displays at each site
  • Employee awareness sessions and Internal Committee member training
  • External member support, including appointment paperwork
  • Annual report preparation for submission to the District Officer
  • Review of the complaints register and timelines

Who it applies to

The Internal Committee requirement applies to workplaces with 10 or more employees. The policy, awareness and display duties apply more widely. We confirm the position for your business during the review.

Options

  • POSH starter kit, one-time
  • External member and annual filing, yearly
  • Training session, per session

How the audit runs

1

Registrations, the last three months of filings, a sample wage register and vendor licences.

2

Each point is checked against the records and the current rules. Gaps are noted, not guessed.

3

A written summary with a Green, Amber or Red rating for each point, and the top three fixes.

4

  • Your details are used only for this review.

Home / FAQ
FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Do you file returns for us?

On the monthly plans we prepare and file PF and ESIC returns, and handle Professional Tax and LWF. Your plan sets the exact scope, and you approve each submission.

Do you need our bank or portal passwords?

No. We work from the documents you share and the access you authorise in writing. We never ask for passwords by WhatsApp.

Do you handle POSH too?

Yes. Internal Committee set-up, policy, awareness training and the annual report are part of our governance offering, as add-ons or within higher plans.

What is the 15-point audit?

A focused review of 15 statutory points across filings, Tamil Nadu obligations and contractor governance. You receive a written report with priority fixes. New clients receive it free as part of their first review.

How do you handle employee data?

Only for the service you have agreed. Data is stored securely with restricted access and deleted on request, subject to legal retention rules.

What do you need from us each month?

Wage registers, attendance, PF and ESIC challans and receipts, and vendor licences, by the 23rd of the month.

Is your review a guarantee against notices?

No. Our reviews rely on the documents provided by you and your vendors. They reduce risk and give you evidence, but they are not a legal opinion or certificate.

Do you work outside Tamil Nadu?

Yes. We work with businesses in other states too. State-specific rules, such as Professional Tax and Shops and Establishments, are applied for each location.
